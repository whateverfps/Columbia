BUILD VS BUY · SCENARIO MODEL

Own the capability.
Buy expertise selectively.

The strategic decision is not simply whether to hire one person or hire a consulting firm. It is whether Columbia wants the AI program's operating knowledge, roadmap and iteration loop to live inside the company—or outside it.

Modeled conclusion: place accountability for the AI program inside Columbia, then use external specialists where they add leverage. The model does not argue that consultants have no value; it argues against outsourcing the core capability.

THE DECISION PRINCIPLE

Do not outsource the learning loop.

The most valuable asset created during an AI program is not a single application. It is the accumulated understanding of Columbia's workflows, data, controls, users, failures and successful patterns. Keeping the program owner inside the company lets that knowledge compound.

Build the ownership internally.Buy products where mature products solve the problem. Bring in specialists where their bench creates leverage. Keep strategy, prioritization, governance, adoption and institutional learning accountable inside Columbia.
