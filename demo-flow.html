Enterprise AI — Demo Flow

Goal: show, don't pitch. The UI establishes the enterprise vision; the role suites make it concrete; Chief proves the capability; their problem turns the interview into collaboration.

1. Launch

“I thought instead of spending our time talking about what an AI program could look like, I’d build something we could work through together.”

Open index.html and let the landing page breathe for a moment. Then click Enter the experience and move into the Enterprise Suite.

2. Enterprise Suite

Spend only a few minutes here. Show the company layer, role suites, enterprise brain and governance. The point: existing systems stay systems of record; AI becomes a governed intelligence layer across them.

3. Role Suites

Open PM/Super first. Show how the interface changes around the job. Briefly open Finance to prove this is not one construction chatbot with a different label.

4. Chief Live

Leave the concept UI and use the real product. Run exactly three disciplined tasks:

  1. Review a real drawing for coordination issues.
  2. Find and explain the governing specification with source evidence.
  3. Calculate CAT 6A footage from the drawing set, showing assumptions and method.

5. Turn It Around

“That’s one example from my world. Give me one process at Columbia that creates friction.”

Listen. Map the people, process, systems, data, decision rights, risk and measurable outcome. Do not force AI where it does not fit.

6. Close

“This is the part that interests me. Not deploying AI for the sake of AI—building the capability inside the company to repeatedly find these problems, prove solutions, and scale the ones that actually work.”
