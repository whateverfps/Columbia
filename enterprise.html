GOVERNANCE AS PRODUCT

Move fast inside visible guardrails.

Governance is part of the operating system—not a policy document added after deployment.

Identity + permissions

AI access follows the user's existing enterprise identity and least-privilege authorization.

Source authority

Systems of record outrank contextual sources and every important source has an owner.

Human decision rights

High-impact decisions stay explicitly assigned to accountable people.

AI use registry

Material uses have a purpose, owner, risk classification and lifecycle state.

Data boundaries

Approved data classes and model destinations are visible before deployment.

Evaluation gates

Quality, reliability and risk are tested against use-case-specific criteria.

Auditability

Important retrieval, tool actions and decisions can be traced and reviewed.

Lifecycle ownership

Production AI has a named owner responsible for monitoring, change and retirement.
