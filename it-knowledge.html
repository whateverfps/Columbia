GOVERNANCE AS PRODUCT

Guardrails people can actually work inside.

Governance should speed up safe experimentation, not exist only as a policy document.

Identity + least privilege

User access follows existing enterprise identity and authorization.

Source authority

Systems of record outrank contextual sources and every source has an owner.

Human decision rights

High-impact decisions stay explicitly assigned to accountable people.

AI use registry

Every material use has an owner, purpose, risk level and lifecycle state.

Data boundaries

Approved data classes and model destinations are visible before deployment.

Evaluation gates

Quality and safety are tested against use-case-specific acceptance criteria.

Auditability

Important retrieval, tool actions and decisions can be traced and reviewed.

Lifecycle ownership

Production AI has a named owner responsible for monitoring, change and retirement.
